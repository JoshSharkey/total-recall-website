**In plain terms:** the Ask Theo app is the window into your brain. It lives at
**asktheo.totalrecallhq.com**, it opens in any browser on any device, and it is where
**[theo](/docs/who-is-theo/)** works at full strength.

theo also rides along in Telegram, Slack, and WhatsApp, and your brain plugs into other AI
tools. Those are great for a quick question on the move. The app is where you see everything
at once.

## What is in the app

Some sections are always there, because they are the product. The rest you switch on when you
want them.

Always on:

- **Today**: what needs you right now, pulled together from everywhere.
- **Daily Brief**: your morning read of what matters. See **[Daily brief](/docs/daily-brief/)**.
- **Goals**: what you are working toward, tracked against what actually happened.
- **Synthesis**: the patterns and blind spots theo finds on its own, across work, health, and
  life. See **[Synthesis](/docs/synthesis/)**.
- **People**: a personal CRM of everyone you know, who to reconnect with, and a prep sheet
  before every call.

Switch on what you want:

- **Tasks**: a board that fills itself from your email, calendar, and conversations, and that
  theo can close on your behalf.
- **Health**: recovery, sleep, labs, and trends, read in context with the rest of your life.
- **Finance, Content, Docs, Travel, Archive**: the rest of your world, in the same place.

## Asking theo anything

Ask is not a separate page. Anywhere in the app you press **Cmd+K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) and an
ask box opens over whatever you were looking at: one question in plain language, an answer
grounded in everything you know, with the sources attached so you can check them. You do not
lose your place. See **[How search works](/docs/search/)**.

That box is the "Ask Theo" the app is named for.

## Setting it up

The app is also where setup happens. A guided wizard walks you through connecting your
sources, one at a time, in plain language: your documents, email, calendar, messages,
meetings, and health data. You choose what comes in, and you can change your mind later.
See **[Plans](/plans/)** for what each tier includes.

## The app, and "Ask theo" in Slack

Two things share the name, so here is the difference:

- **The Ask Theo app** is the web app described on this page. It is the full product, and you
  will also see it called simply the theo app.
- **"Ask theo" in Slack** is a small Slack app you can install into your workspace so you can
  message theo without leaving Slack. It is one of the channels theo shows up in, not a
  separate product, and it reads the same brain.

Both talk to the same brain, and neither one holds a copy of your data. See
**[Your data & ownership](/docs/ownership/)**.

## Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The app works in a mobile browser, and theo is also reachable from the Claude app on
your phone once you add your brain as a connector. One thing to know: reaching your brain
from your phone means the brain has to be awake when your laptop is not, so it needs to run
somewhere always on. See **[Using it from your phone](/docs/phone-access/)** for the detail.

## Who can get into it

Only you. Sign-in is restricted to your approved account, and one brain belongs to one
person. See **[Privacy & backups](/docs/backups-privacy/)**.
