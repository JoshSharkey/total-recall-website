**In plain terms:** Total Recall is the brain. **theo** is the agent built on it, the one you
actually talk to. The brain is the memory; theo is the intelligence that uses it.

If that split sounds abstract, think of it this way. The brain is everything you know, stored
and connected. theo is the colleague who has read all of it, never forgets any of it, and is
awake at 6am to tell you what matters today.

## Why theo works when other assistants do not

Most assistants start every conversation from nothing. You spend the first five minutes
explaining who you are, what the project is, and who the people are. theo starts from your
whole world: your work, your people, your health, your ideas, your decisions and how they
turned out.

That context is the entire difference. An assistant with no memory can only answer questions.
An assistant with your memory can tell you what you are missing.

## What theo does for you

- **Briefs you.** A morning read of what matters: people, decisions, follow-ups, and signals
  worth your attention. See **[Daily brief](/docs/daily-brief/)**.
- **Knows your people.** A personal CRM that tells you who to reconnect with and preps you
  before every call.
- **Answers anything.** Ask in plain language and get answers grounded in everything you know,
  with the receipts. See **[How search works](/docs/search/)**.
- **Connects the dots.** Synthesis across work, health, and life, surfacing patterns and blind
  spots on its own. See **[Synthesis](/docs/synthesis/)**.
- **Takes action, when you want it to.** Closes tasks, marks goals, drafts and sends, and
  nudges you at the right moment.
- **Watches your health.** Recovery, sleep, and trends in context with the rest of your life.

## theo learns you

This is the part that compounds. Every action theo takes is written down as a **trace**: the
signal it saw, the call it made, the reasoning, and how confident it was. Think of it as a
receipt for every decision.

1. **It retrieves its priors.** Before acting, theo pulls up similar past decisions and how
   they scored. It rereads the logbook before making the same kind of call again.
2. **It acts, and shows its work.** Every action comes with that receipt, so you can see why.
3. **You label it by talking.** "Good catch." "No, that is not done yet." Ordinary corrections
   become permanent labels on the exact decision they refer to. One sentence from you is the
   whole training loop.
4. **It reinforces, or it decays.** Confirmed calls get reinforced. Corrected calls become
   lessons that block repeats. Stale context fades on its own.

The practical result: the mistake you flag today does not come back next month, and the theo
you talk to in three months is measurably better than the one you talk to today.

## Where you talk to theo

theo is not locked to one window:

- **The theo app** is theo at full strength, with every section of your brain in front of
  you. See **[What is the theo app](/docs/theo-app/)**.
- **Your messaging channels** put theo where you already are: Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp.
- **Any AI you already use** can reach your brain directly, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini,
  and any MCP-capable tool. See **[Using it from your phone](/docs/phone-access/)**.

## Is theo one agent, or mine alone?

Yours alone. One brain belongs to one person, and theo's memory of you is your brain, not a
shared pool. Your information is never mixed with anyone else's. See
**[Your data & ownership](/docs/ownership/)**.
