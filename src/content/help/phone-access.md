title: "Using it from your phone"
description: "Talk to your brain from the Claude app on your phone by adding it as a connector, so your second brain is with you anywhere, not just at your desk."
---

**In plain terms:** your brain is most useful when it is in your pocket. By adding
it to the Claude app as a connector, you can ask it anything from your phone:
before a meeting, in the car, anywhere. This is the feature that makes it feel
like a product rather than a desk tool.

## What you can do with it

- Ask the brain anything from the Claude mobile app.
- Pull up a person's profile before you walk into a room.
- Capture and recall on the go.

## How it works (simply)

The brain runs a secure connection that the Claude app can talk to. You add it
once as a custom connector and sign in with your approved account; from then on it
shows up in the Claude app under your login. Sign-in is restricted to approved
accounts, so only you can reach your brain.

## What it needs (the important part)

- The brain must be **reachable when your computer is off**, which means it runs
  somewhere always-on: either an always-on computer at home exposed securely, or a
  cloud-hosted setup. A laptop that sleeps cannot answer your phone.

## Honest limits

- Phone access requires an always-on brain. A purely local, sometimes-asleep
  laptop brain is not reachable on the go.
- Some background "memory" features that need an always-on Mac (like text memory)
  are separate from phone access; you can have phone access without them and vice
  versa.
