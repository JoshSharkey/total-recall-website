---# Reminders & relationship cadence

**In plain terms:** the brain helps you be a better friend and colleague by
remembering the things you mean to keep up with: birthdays, anniversaries, and
simply staying in touch with people who matter. It nudges you before the date,
not after, and can suggest what to say or give.

## What you can do with it

- "Whose birthday is coming up?"
- Get a heads-up a week before, and again the day of.
- "Suggest a gift for my brother" based on what the brain knows about him.
- Nudges to reconnect with people you have not spoken to in a while.

## How it works (simply)

Birthdays and key dates live on people's profiles (a child's birthday, for
example, is attached to the parent so the reminder reaches you, not a child with
no contact info). A reminder engine looks ahead and fires nudges on a schedule,
delivered through whichever assistant you use (for example a Telegram message).
For some kinds of outreach, like handwritten cards, it can ride the same reminder
cadence.

## What it needs

- A loaded CRM (the more people and dates it knows, the more useful the nudges).
- A delivery channel for the nudges (see [Proactive agents](proactive-agents.md)).

## Honest limits

- It reminds and drafts; you still decide and send. It is an assistant, not an
  autopilot for your relationships.
