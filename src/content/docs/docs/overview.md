**In plain terms:** Total Recall is a private memory for one person. It quietly collects the things
you already deal with every day, your documents, notes, emails, texts, meetings, the people you
know, your health, into one place, and then connects and reasons across all of it. Think of it as a
second brain that never forgets and that actively helps you think.

It is **yours alone**. One brain belongs to one person. Your information is never mixed with anyone
else's.

## The brain and the agent

- **Total Recall** is the **brain**, your private memory and the synthesis that runs on top of it.
- **Theo** is the **agent** built on that brain, the intelligence you actually talk to.

## It is more than search

The point isn't only asking questions about your stuff. Because your brain holds all of your
context, personal and professional, it does far more than answer. It connects the dots across every
corner of your life: surfacing patterns, catching blind spots, pressure-testing your decisions, and
briefing you every morning on what matters. That is **[synthesis](/docs/synthesis/)**, and it is the
heart of the product.

## Where you use it

Your brain is portable, so you are not locked to one place:

- **Theo, your agent** — at full strength in the Theo app, and with you in messaging channels like
  Telegram, Slack, and WhatsApp.
- **Any AI you already use** — plug your brain into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any MCP-capable tool.

## Private by design

Total Recall is built private-first. See **[Privacy & backups](/docs/backups-privacy/)** for how your
information is stored and protected.
