---# Mission Control app

**In plain terms:** most of the brain is something you talk to. Mission Control is
something you can *look at*: a personal dashboard that shows what is in your brain
and lets you work with it visually, instead of only by asking questions.

## What you can do with it

- See and manage your **tasks** in one board.
- Browse your **personal CRM**: people, profiles, upcoming dates.
- Read your **daily briefs** and synthesis.
- Track your **goals** and your **health** (charts, medications).
- Use a built-in **chatbot** to query and update the brain right from the app.

## How it works (simply)

It is a companion app that reads and writes the same brain. What you see in the
dashboard is the same data your assistant uses; editing it in the app updates the
brain, and vice versa.

## What it needs

- A running brain to connect to.
- It is offered as an add-on alongside the core brain.

## Honest limits

- It is a view and a control panel for the brain, not a separate product with its
  own data.
