Total Recall is built so that your brain is genuinely **yours**, not something you rent or hand
over. Here is what that means in practice.

## Yours alone

One brain belongs to one person. Your data is never pooled or mixed with anyone else's. There is no
shared model learning from your life.

## Take it anywhere

Your brain is portable. Plug it into the AI channels you already use, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or
your messaging apps, and unplug any of them whenever you want. You are never locked into a single
vendor or interface.

## Yours to keep, or delete

Your information is yours to export, move, or remove. Every idea, message, and data point stays
under your control, and you can take it with you or delete it on your terms.

## Private by design

Total Recall runs privately. Your world is never sold, shared, or turned into anyone else's training
data. See **[Privacy & backups](/docs/backups-privacy/)** for how your data is stored and protected.
