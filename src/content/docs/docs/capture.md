---# Capture (dreams, journal, ideas, brainstorms)

**In plain terms:** the brain gives you a frictionless way to capture fleeting
thoughts. Speak or jot a quick note, say what kind it is, and it gets filed
automatically: a dream, a journal entry, an idea, a brainstorm. No deciding where
it goes; you just capture, and it lands in the right place and becomes searchable.

## What you can do with it

- Speak a dream when you wake up and have it filed as a dream entry.
- Capture an idea on the go without breaking flow.
- Keep a running journal that the brain can later reflect patterns from.
- Brainstorm out loud and keep the thread.

## How it works (simply)

You capture with a voice recorder or quick note. A simple spoken or typed cue at
the start (for example saying "dream" or "idea" first) tells the brain which
bucket it belongs in. It routes the capture to the matching place, tags it, and
ingests it like any other note.

## What it needs

- A capture device or app feeding the brain (for example a voice recorder that
  drops transcripts into the source folder).

## Honest limits

- Deeper analysis across many entries (patterns in mood, recurring themes) gets
  better with volume; a handful of entries will not reveal much yet.
