---# Personal CRM

**In plain terms:** the brain keeps a private profile for each person and company
you know. Not a corporate sales database with job titles and not much else, but a
*relationship* memory: how you know them, their kids' names, what they care
about, what you last talked about, the things that make a relationship real.

Profiles build themselves. As the brain reads your emails, texts, meetings, and
notes, it quietly adds what it learns to the right person.

## What you can do with it

- "Summarize everything I know about Jane."
- "Who do I know at that company?"
- "What did Mark and I last discuss?"
- "Remind me how I know this person before my call."

## How it works (simply)

The brain stores two things: **entities** (the people, companies, and projects
you know) and **facts** about them (role, employer, family, interests, life
events, how they relate to you). Facts come in with their source attached, so you
can always see where something came from. New facts arrive as "provisional" until
confirmed, so nothing pollutes a profile silently.

When two records turn out to be the same person, they can be merged. When the
brain is unsure who a name refers to, it asks you with a one-tap prompt rather
than guessing.

## Honest limits

- Quality tracks input. The more of your real world the brain sees, the richer
  the profiles. Importing a clean contacts list at setup gives it a strong start.
- It is a personal brain, not a shared team CRM.
