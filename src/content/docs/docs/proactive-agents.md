---# Proactive agents

**In plain terms:** the brain does not only answer when spoken to. It can have an
assistant that reaches out to *you*: a birthday reminder, a morning brief, a "you
have not talked to this person in a while" nudge, or a heads-up that something
needs attention. It is the difference between a library and a helpful colleague.

## What you can do with it

- Get reminders and nudges pushed to you (for example via Telegram).
- Receive your daily brief without asking.
- Answer one-tap questions from the brain (for example "is this 'Chris' the same
  Chris you already know?") right in the chat.
- Have a single assistant that can also take actions across your connected tools.

## How it works (simply)

A small always-on assistant watches for things worth telling you about and sends
them to a chat app you already use. The same assistant can take actions (draft,
send, schedule) under rules you set. Telegram and Slack are the practical channels
today.

## What it needs

- An always-on place for the assistant to run.
- A chat channel (Telegram or Slack) connected to it.

## Honest limits

- Each chat channel is its own setup. iMessage as a channel is fragile and
  avoided; Telegram or Slack are the recommended choices.
- Proactive messaging is a build per channel, not a flip-a-switch setting on a
  brand-new brain.
