---# Email memory

**In plain terms:** the brain can learn from your email the same way it learns
from texts. As messages come and go, it picks out the things worth remembering
(who someone is, what they are working on, what you agreed to) and files them onto
the right person or project. You get the memory without doing the filing.

This is one of the features that makes the brain feel like it "just knows things"
over time.

## What you can do with it

- "What did this client say they needed?"
- "Catch me up on the thread with the legal team."
- People's profiles fill in automatically from email (roles, companies, context).

## How it works (simply)

On a schedule, the brain reads new mail, extracts the meaningful facts, attaches
them to the right people, and keeps the rest out. Like everything in the CRM, new
facts arrive as "provisional" with their source attached, so you can trust where
they came from.

## What it needs

- Today this runs as a background job tied to **Gmail / Google Workspace** on an
  always-on computer. It is the richest version of email memory.
- Separately, your assistant can also take **live email actions** (draft, send,
  search) through Claude's own connectors, which work from anywhere, including
  Outlook. That is acting on email, as opposed to remembering it.

## Honest limits

- The automatic "remember my email" background job is Gmail-and-Mac-based today.
  Outlook email *memory* (not actions) is a future build.
- It does not yet run on a cloud-only brain.
