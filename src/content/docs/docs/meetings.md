---# Meetings

**In plain terms:** your meetings stop evaporating. Recordings and transcripts get
stored, the action items get pulled out, and before your next call the brain can
brief you on the person, the history, and what is open, so you walk in prepared.

## What you can do with it

- "Pull the action items from yesterday's call."
- "What did we decide in the last meeting with this client?"
- "Prep me before my 2pm" (who they are, recent context, open items).
- Search across past meetings by topic.

## How it works (simply)

Transcripts (for example from a recorder like Plaud, or from your call tooling)
land in the brain as meeting pages. The brain reads them to extract decisions,
action items, and facts about the people involved, attaching those facts to the
right profiles. Meeting prep then draws on the whole brain, not just the meeting.

## What it needs

- A source of transcripts (a recorder or a meeting-notes tool that exports text).

## Honest limits

- It can only work from transcripts it receives; it does not silently record
  anything on its own.
- Coverage is only as good as how consistently your meetings get transcribed.
