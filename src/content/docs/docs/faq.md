## What is Total Recall?

Total Recall is a private, single-owner second brain. It collects the things you
already deal with every day (documents, notes, emails, texts, meetings, the people
you know, your health data) into one place, and lets you ask questions about all of
it in plain language. Think of it as a memory that never forgets and that you can
talk to. See **[the overview](/docs/overview/)**.

## What is Theo?

Theo is the app you talk to. Total Recall is the brain (the memory and reasoning
layer); Theo is the face of it — where you ask questions, read your daily brief,
review people, and act on what the brain surfaces.

## Is my data private? Is it mixed with other people's?

No. One brain belongs to one person. Your information is never combined with anyone
else's. Total Recall is built private-first; see **[Backups & privacy](/docs/backups-privacy/)**
for how your data is stored and protected.

## What can I ask it?

Anything you'd ask someone who remembered your whole life, for example:

- "Summarize everything I know about Jane and her company."
- "What did we decide last week about pricing?"
- "Pull the action items from my last call with the design team."
- "What's quietly driving my best months?"

See **[Search & retrieval](/docs/search/)**.

## Where does the information come from?

From sources you connect: your documents and notes, email, messages, Slack,
meetings, calendar, health data, and more. You choose what to bring in. Browse the
**Capabilities** in the sidebar to see each source.

## Can I use it from my phone?

Yes. See **[Phone access](/docs/phone-access/)**.

## Does it just store things, or does it think?

Both. Beyond answering questions, it produces a **[daily brief](/docs/daily-brief/)**,
runs **[life synthesis](/docs/life-synthesis/)** across everything it knows, and can
act through **[proactive agents](/docs/proactive-agents/)**.
