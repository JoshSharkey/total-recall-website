---# Writing to the brain

**In plain terms:** the brain is not read-only. When you are chatting with your
assistant and something worth keeping comes up ("remember that this client
prefers morning calls"), it can be written straight into the brain on the spot,
from any of your AI surfaces. The brain gets smarter as you use it, not only when
a background job runs.

## What you can do with it

- "Remember that Jane is now VP of Product." (saved as a fact on her profile)
- "Add a page summarizing today's strategy session."
- Capture a decision or a note mid-conversation and have it persist.

## How it works (simply)

The brain exposes a set of safe "write" tools that your connected assistants
(Claude on your computer or phone, a Telegram assistant, the dashboard chat) can
use: add a page, add a fact about someone, add an observation, or remove a page.
Writes are permission-gated, so only your authorized surfaces can change the
brain.

## What it needs

- Your assistant connected to the brain with write access enabled.

## Honest limits

- Write access is gated for safety; a casual or unauthorized connection can read
  but not change the brain.
