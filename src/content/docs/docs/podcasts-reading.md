---# Podcasts & reading

**In plain terms:** the things you listen to and read are part of your thinking,
so they should be part of your brain. Podcasts can be mined for the people and
ideas mentioned, and your reading lists and books are kept so you can act on them.

## What you can do with it

- "What did that guest say about hiring?"
- "Add my entrepreneurship reading list and send it to a friend."
- "What book should I read next?"
- People mentioned in a podcast get connected to their profiles (without
  inventing new people who are not already known).

## How it works (simply)

Podcast transcripts are processed to extract facts and connections, attaching them
to people already in your CRM rather than creating speculative new entries.
Reading lists and books are stored as structured entries you can query and share.

## Honest limits

- Podcast extraction is conservative on people: it links to existing contacts
  rather than guessing at new ones.
