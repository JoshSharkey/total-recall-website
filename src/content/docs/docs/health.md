---# Health & fitness

**In plain terms:** the brain can hold your health picture in one place: your
wearable data (sleep, recovery, strain) and the medications and supplements you
take. That lets you ask questions across it and notice patterns you would
otherwise miss.

## What you can do with it

- "How has my sleep been this week?"
- "What's my recovery trend lately?"
- "What supplements am I taking and at what dose?"
- Spot patterns between how you feel, your habits, and the data.

## How it works (simply)

- **Wearables:** your full history is pulled in and refreshed daily, then made
  searchable, with charts in the dashboard. WHOOP is supported today, with room to
  add others (such as Oura) over time.
- **Medications & supplements:** an editable list (status, dosage, notes) you keep
  in the dashboard, also backed up as readable pages in the brain.

## What it needs

- A connected wearable account for the automatic daily pull.
- The dashboard ([Mission Control](mission-control.md)) for the health charts and
  the medications grid.

## Honest limits

- This is for your own awareness and pattern-spotting. It is not medical advice
  and not a clinical record.
